# Research Problem
## 1. Industrial Context
The German automative industry relies on complex inetnational supply networks for the procurement of critical electronic components.

Components such as microcontrollers, power semiconducters, and sensors paly essential roels in modern vehicle systems. 
Disruptions in their supply can affect production continuty, particularly when alternative sources are limited or difficult to qualify.

At the same time, manufacturers face financial and operational constraints when implementing resiliencce measures. 
Decissions regarding safety stock, alternative sourcing, and supplier diversification therefore involve trade-offd between resilience, cost, and resource availability.

## 2.Problem Statement
Automative manufacturers face the challenge of managing supply vulnerabilities across multiple critical components while operating under limited financial and operational resources.

Existing resilience measures, includung safety stock and alternative sourcing, involve different cists, lead times,and capacity requirements. Applying these mear´sures uniformly across all components may therefore result in inefficient resource allocation.
The central problem addressed by AutoResilience is the lack of an integrated decison-support approach that connects component-level supply risk assessment with resilience startgy selection an d resource allocation under operational and financial constraints.

## 3.Research Question
Main Research Question
How can automative manufacturers identify critical supply vulnerabilities, select appropriate resilience strategies, and optimally allocate limited finacial and operational resources to minimitze disruption-related costs across multiple critical components?
Supporting Research Questions:
1. How can external supply exposure and component critically be assessed using publicly available data and structured indicators?
2. How can alternative resilience strategies, such as safety stock and dual sourcing, be evaluated under different distruption scenario?
3. How can procurement and resilience investments be optimized under budget and supplier-capacity constraints?
4. What trade-offs emerge between total cost, inventory investment,and supply availability across alternative sourcing startegies?
